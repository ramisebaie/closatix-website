Arabic and English

Book, reschedule and cancel

Read real availability, create the appointment and handle changes within the permissions you approve.

HVAC-specific qualification

Understand unit type, issue, quantity and urgency, then classify repair, maintenance, installation, replacement or commercial work.

Service-area validation

Check city and district before booking, and route out-of-area requests for review without making a false promise.

Human transfer and escalation

Transfer when the caller asks, the opportunity is high-value, the customer is upset or the AI is uncertain.

CRM and calendar sync

Create or update contacts, appointments, outcomes and summaries after technical compatibility is confirmed.

SMS and WhatsApp confirmations

Send approved appointment confirmation and pre-visit instructions through the selected channel.

Summaries and transcripts

Store reason, data, result and summary. Full recordings or transcripts are enabled only where appropriate and lawful.

Safety and no invented answers

Do not invent pricing, services or appointments. Escalate possible electrical or fire hazards and keep advice limited.

Operational reporting

Track calls, qualified requests, bookings, transfers and no-book reasons to improve the flow over time.

Missed-opportunity recovery

Add a compliant follow-up path for missed calls or qualified requests that did not book.
