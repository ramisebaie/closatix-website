The Real Bottleneck

Most real estate teams don't need more software.
They need faster action.

None of the following is a people problem. It's what happens when lead volume grows faster than the process behind it — and every one of these steps still runs on someone's attention.

  • 01

    Leads arrive from everywhere

    Website forms, landing pages, portals, ad campaigns and referrals — each in a different format, each needing a different next step.

  • 02

    Every inquiry waits for a human to read it

    Before anyone can act, someone has to open the message, interpret it and decide what it's worth.

  • 03

    Follow-up happens later than intended

    Showings, calls and paperwork fill the day. The response goes out when there's time, not when the interest is highest.

  • 04

    Serious buyers sit next to casual browsers

    Without a consistent qualification step, a financing-ready prospect looks the same in the inbox as a general question.

  • 05

    CRM records drift out of date

    Manual entry is inconsistent by nature. Fields get skipped, notes stay in someone's head, reporting loses its meaning.

  • 06

    Opportunities quietly disappear

    Nobody decides to drop a lead. It simply moves down the list until it stops being visible.

  • 07

    Administration replaces selling

    Copying, sorting, tagging and chasing take the hours that should go to conversations with clients.

CLOSATIX takes those disconnected steps and runs them as one workflow — from the moment a lead arrives to the moment it's on someone's calendar.

How It Works

Four steps. One uninterrupted path.

The sequence below runs on every incoming lead, in the same order, every time — without anyone having to start it.

01

Capture

Incoming leads enter the automated workflow from your website, landing pages, forms, booking pages and other connected sources — in one consistent structure.

02

Qualify

AI reads the relevant details — intent, property type, location, budget, timeline and financing status — and determines whether the opportunity is Hot, Warm or Cold.

03

Automate

CRM records are created or updated, the right people are notified, and follow-up begins in the form that matches the lead's readiness.

04

Act

Your team opens the day knowing exactly where to focus, while qualified prospects are guided toward booking the next conversation.
