Website privacy

Privacy notice

This notice explains how the current public marketing website handles visitor data. It does not govern any client AI voice system, which requires its own scope, contract and data terms.

Last updated: 26 August 2026

1. Scope

This notice applies to the public SAQR Voice pages hosted as a static marketing website. SAQR Voice is currently a service brand; the legally contracting party is identified in each client proposal or agreement.

2. Data collected by this website

The current website has no internal contact form or proprietary marketing database and does not collect prospect information directly through the page.

The hosting provider may automatically process ordinary technical logs such as IP address, browser type, request time and security events under its own policies.

3. External booking service

Booking buttons take you to the external Cal.com service. Information you choose to submit is processed by that service under its privacy notice and terms. Review the booking page before entering sensitive information.

4. Cookies and analytics

No custom analytics, advertising or behavioural tracking tool is currently installed on this website. External services linked from the site may use their own cookies when you visit them.

5. Data in client voice systems

A client AI voice implementation may process phone numbers, call summaries, service details, appointments, recordings or transcripts where enabled. That processing does not begin automatically through this website. Client documents define:

  • controller, processor and operational responsibilities;
  • data types and processing purposes;
  • required notices and consent;
  • retention and deletion rules;
  • sub-processors and processing locations;
  • security and incident response procedures.

6. Do not send sensitive information

Do not submit financial data, health information, passwords or identity documents through public booking links unless a clearly identified secure route is provided.

7. Retention and deletion

Because the website has no internal lead form or marketing database, it does not currently retain prospect submissions itself. Booking data is governed by the external service settings and can be reviewed or deleted through the channel used.

8. Changes

This notice may change when a form, analytics, official domain or final legal and operating structure is added. The latest revision date will appear above.

9. Contact

For a website privacy request, use the booking link on the site and state clearly that the request concerns privacy. A formal legal contact address will be added when established.
