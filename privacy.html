2. Two different roles

This is the most important thing to understand about how we handle data, because we act in two distinct capacities.

As a controller — visitors to this website

When you visit closatix.com or book a call with us, we decide what we collect and why. That is described in sections 3 to 7.

As a processor — our clients' leads

When we operate the CLOSATIX system for a client, that client's prospect and lead data is processed strictly on that client's instructions. The client is the controller of that data; we are a processor acting on their behalf. We do not use it for our own purposes, we do not sell it, and we do not use it to train AI models. That relationship is governed by our services agreement with the client, not by this policy. If you are a prospect of one of our clients and want your data removed, contact that business directly — they control it.

We use access controls, encryption in transit, scoped credentials limited to what each integration needs, and restrict access to personnel who require it. No system is perfectly secure, but if a breach affects your personal data we will notify you and the relevant authority as required by law.

10. AI processing

Our service uses AI models to assess and prioritise lead enquiries. Lead content is sent to third-party AI providers for that purpose. We use providers on terms that do not permit client data to be used for model training. AI outputs are advisory and are reviewed within our clients' own sales processes — they are not used to make automated decisions producing legal effects.
