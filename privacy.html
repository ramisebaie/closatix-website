Website Privacy Notice

Last updated: August 2026

What this notice covers. This page describes the current behaviour of the public CLOSATIX website. Data processing for a paying client is documented separately in the signed service agreement and onboarding materials.

1. The current website

This is a static marketing website. CLOSATIX does not currently run advertising pixels or a website analytics platform, and the website does not submit a contact or audit form to CLOSATIX servers.

The hosting provider may process basic technical information needed to deliver and secure the site, such as IP address, browser information and request logs, under that provider's own terms.

2. Booking a call

Booking buttons take you to Cal.com, a third-party scheduling service. When you book, the information you enter is processed by Cal.com and shared with the event organiser so the meeting can be scheduled, managed and followed up.

Review Cal.com's Privacy Policy before submitting booking information.

3. Information used by CLOSATIX

When you book a meeting or correspond with CLOSATIX, we may use the details you provide, meeting notes and related messages to:

  • respond to your request and prepare for the meeting;
  • understand your business process and determine whether the service is a fit;
  • prepare a proposal or follow up on the conversation; and
  • maintain reasonable business and security records.

CLOSATIX does not sell personal information or use booking details for third-party advertising.

4. Client lead data

When CLOSATIX delivers an automation service for a client, the client determines the business purpose and approved workflow for its lead data. CLOSATIX processes that information only to deliver and maintain the agreed service, subject to the signed service agreement, authorised access and the client's instructions.

The systems and third-party providers used for a specific implementation are identified during onboarding. Access should be limited to what is necessary for the agreed workflow.

5. Third-party services

The public website may rely on third parties for hosting, scheduling and font delivery. Those providers handle technical or booking information under their own privacy terms. Following an external link means you are leaving this website.

6. Retention and security

Booking and correspondence information is kept only as long as reasonably needed for the sales conversation, business records, security or a resulting client relationship. Client-service data is retained according to the signed agreement and the client's instructions.

No online system can be guaranteed perfectly secure, but access to company systems should use appropriate account controls, limited permissions and multi-factor authentication where available.

7. Changes to this notice

This notice will be updated before CLOSATIX adds a connected website form, analytics platform, advertising technology or any materially different data practice.

8. Contact

To ask a privacy question, use the CLOSATIX booking page and write “Privacy request” in the booking notes.
